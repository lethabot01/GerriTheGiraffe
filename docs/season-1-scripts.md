How to use these scripts

- Each episode script is under 2,500 characters, so you can paste it
  straight into an animation tool. Each one already includes a short
  description of Gerri and Thola.

- Lines marked (pause) leave 2–3 seconds of silence so children at home
  can answer.

- The full sing-along song for each episode follows its script, with
  verses and chorus. All songs are original, so you can use them freely,
  for example in Suno.

- The YouTube title, description and tags for each episode are at the
  end of each episode section.

Character & Style Guide

**Style:** Bright, soft 2D/3D cartoon for preschoolers (ages 3–6).
Rounded shapes, warm colours, gentle lighting, no scary or sharp
imagery. Slow, clear movements and simple backgrounds.

**Gerri the Giraffe:** Main character (female). Tall, soft yellow
giraffe with orange spots, big friendly brown eyes, long eyelashes,
small rounded horns and a light-blue scarf with white stars. Warm,
cheerful, curious, a little dreamy. Dreams of flying to space.

**Thola the Meerkat:** Helper (female). Small sandy-brown meerkat with
round orange goggles on her head and a green tool belt. Quick, bright
and enthusiastic. Says "Eish!" when things go wrong and "Sharp sharp!"
when things go right. Inventor who builds Gerri's spaceship.

**Zuri the Zebra:** Cheerful, helpful little zebra (from Episode 9).

**Bongani the Baby Elephant:** Shy, gentle and very strong (from Episode
9).

**Kiki the Hornbill:** Chatty black-and-white bird with a big
yellow-orange beak who talks-really-fast (from Episode 9).

**Setting:** Thola's workshop, a cosy wooden shed under a big acacia
tree on a golden savannah, with tools, cardboard, paint pots and a
chalkboard. At night: a deep blue sky full of stars and a big glowing
Moon.

**The rocket:** Handmade from cardboard, tins and bottle caps. Silver
and red with a round window. It changes and grows taller through the
season.

Episode 1 – Gerri Looks Up

**Runtime:** about 6 minutes **Learning goal:** big and small

Script

*Gerri: tall yellow giraffe, orange spots, blue star scarf. Thola: small
meerkat, orange goggles, green tool belt. Bright soft cartoon, preschool
style.*

**SCENE 1 – INTRO (0:30):** *Night sky. Gerri gazes at stars; Thola pops
up. Theme song: "Look up high, way up high, Gerri's dreaming of the sky!
With Thola building, step by step, we'll reach the stars – we're not
done yet!"*

**SCENE 2 (1 min):** *Gerri lies in grass under stars.*

**GERRI:** Hi friends! I'm Gerri! I dream of flying to the stars. Are
stars close or far? (pause) Far!

**THOLA:** I'm Thola, an inventor! I'll build you a spaceship!

**SCENE 3 (1 min):** *Workshop under acacia tree. Thola builds a tiny
cardboard rocket. Gerri pushes her head in – only her head fits; the box
sits on her head like a hat.*

**GERRI:** Thola, something's wrong!

**THOLA:** Eish!

**SCENE 4 (1 min):** *Labels "SMALL" on box, "BIG" on Gerri.*

**THOLA:** Friends, is the rocket big or small? (pause) Small! Is Gerri
big or small? (pause) Big!

*Quick cuts: ladybird/tree, pebble/rock, Thola/Gerri, each labelled
small/big.*

**SCENE 5 – SONG (1.5 min):** *Gerri and Thola dance.*

> *"A ladybird is small, small, small, a tree is big and tall, tall,
> tall. Reach, reach, reach for the stars, up past the Moon and up past
> Mars! Big or small, we try it all – I'm reaching for the stars!"*

**SCENE 6 (45 sec):** *Thola draws a bigger rocket on a chalkboard.*

**GERRI:** Should the next rocket be big or small, friends? (pause) BIG!

**SCENE 7 (30 sec):** *Night, both under stars.*

**THOLA:** Today we learned big and small!

**GERRI:** One day I'll fly up there. Bye, friends!

**THOLA:** Sharp sharp! Fade out on a twinkling star.

Sing-along: I'm Reaching for the Stars

**Verse 2**

> My friend Thola's small and smart,
