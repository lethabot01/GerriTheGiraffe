    Meet the cast

    Bright, soft cartoon style for preschoolers: rounded shapes, warm colours, gentle lighting.

    Gerri the Giraffe

    Tall, soft yellow giraffe with orange spots, big brown eyes and a light-blue scarf with white stars. Warm, cheerful, curious and a little dreamy. She dreams of flying to space.

    Thola the Meerkat

    Small golden-tan inventor with round orange goggles and a green tool belt. Quick, bright and enthusiastic. Says “Eish!” when things go wrong and “Sharp sharp!” when they go right.

    Kiki the Hornbill

    Chatty black-and-white bird with a big yellow-orange beak who talks-really-fast. Joins in Episode 9.

    Bongani the Baby Elephant

    Shy, gentle and very strong. Lifts the rocket onto the launch pad. Joins in Episode 9.

    Zuri the Zebra

    Cheerful, helpful little zebra with a fluffy brown mohawk and a big wave for everyone. Always ready to say “Of course!” Joins in Episode 9.
